# Streak audit, phase 1 (21 Streak): measurements, no proposal accepted
**Assumption (lead, 4:32 PM):** every streak-point figure below uses the RULES.md values (2nd +250, 3rd +500, 4th +1,000; a run can never exceed 4 with 4 columns). Landon's app (Triumph Arcade 21 Blackjack) folds streak points into Score on its end screen, so these values are NOT confirmed. If the true values were different: all counts (runs, setups, declines, windows, busts) stay the same; every bonus-point figure scales linearly with the true values; the champion's own choices would also change (its network was trained with these values), so decline/gap numbers are only valid for this value table. With half the values, the bonus would be about 740/game and the lost-window upper bound below about 100/game (calculated, linear scaling).
Labels: **MEASURED** = counted in logged games (game count and SE given). **HINDSIGHT** = label that uses the real future order of a finished game, offline only. **CALCULATED** = arithmetic on measured numbers. **GUESS**.
Code, raw logs and outputs: `experiments/streak-measure/` (README there). Data used: A = shipped52 on set1000, 1,000 games (Theorist's per-decision log `regret_map/s1k_champ_all.jsonl`; it is **shipped52, not deeper:16**; score 8,872 +- 38 reproduces the Referee's). B = deeper:16 on 150 training games (seeds 3,500,000,001 + g*7919, my driver, 150 games, all played; B score 8,814 +- 112 on those seeds, 150-game SE is large). Per-move logs of the Referee's official deeper:16 run do not exist (CSVs hold score/busts/perfect only). The Learner's run1/run2 are not used for distributions: their games are perturbed on purpose (bust-eps 0.02, bust-seek 0.03) and only log positions at <=16 cards.

## 2. Missed streak opportunities
**Declined scoring placements while a streak was live (previous card scored), A:** scoring placement available in 4,745 decisions, taken 4,510 (95.0%), declined 235 (5.0%): 52-33 cards left 31 of 996 (3.1%), 32-17 59 of 1,373 (4.3%), 16-1 145 of 2,376 (6.1%; 124 of the 145 were decided by the exact solver). With a streak of 2+ live (the 3rd or 4th scoring card, bonus 500/1,000): 17 declines in 1,144 chances (1.5%). The champion's own search values say the declines were deliberate: value of the best scoring candidate minus the chosen one = median -206 / -143 / -154 (52-33 / 32-17 / 16-1; mean immediate points given up 1,057 / 1,054 / 844). B shows the same (3 of 146, 7 of 187, 29 of 383; 26 of the 29 exact). A decline is a loss only if the champion's valuation is wrong; the test of that is section 3.
**Hindsight lost windows (HINDSIGHT, A):** from the real state at decision t, the longest chain of consecutive scoring placements with the real next cards (Lreach). A window = a state where prev streak + Lreach >= 3 but the realized run was shorter. 250 windows in 1,000 games (0.25/game; 206 games have one; 234 were a 3-streak, 16 a 4-streak), B 0.23/game. The first decision where the chain broke was **a build (224) or a non-scoring Stay (26); never a bust (0 of 250)**; in 245 of 250 a scoring placement existed in the actual state (the champion declined it). Bonus points inside those windows: 197 +- 15 per game (A), by cards left at the break 22 / 45 / 131 (52-33 / 32-17 / 16-1; B: 10 / 30 / 133). This is an upper bound, not a loss: it ignores what the declined placement was worth (section above: median own gap -150 to -200 points) and that the exact solver made 83% of the late declines. Only 67 per game of it is at more than 16 cards left.
**Setups (A, 1,000 games), "strong" = a column in which >=15% of the unseen cards score:** per game 16.1 decisions with 0 strong columns, 12.5 with 1, 6.6 with 2, 2.5 with 3, 0.4 with 4 (no streak live). Realized run >=3 from such a state vs the HINDSIGHT reachable: 0 strong 0.3% vs 0.3%; 1 strong 1.3% vs 1.6%; 2 strong 6.2% vs 7.7%; 3 strong 11.8% vs 16.1%; 4 strong 18.4% vs 22.2% (4-run: 8.6% vs 10.1%). B agrees within its SE (3 strong: 14.4% vs 17.2%, 63 setups with 4 strong in 150 games). Setups with >=3 simultaneous strong columns occur 2.9 times per game (streak not live) and convert to a 3-run 13% of the time, a 4-run 3.7% of the time (A; hindsight-reachable 17% and 4.0%). The "any unseen card scores" version (live_cols) is in `out_*.txt` (4 live columns: 6.3 per game, run>=3 8.5%).

## What the measurements say (not a proposal yet)
1. The champion's streak play is not leaking through bust-driven or decline-driven mistakes: runs end for lack of a scoring placement 97.5% of the time, busts 0.15%, declines mostly by the exact solver and by its own judgment. Streak-live positions carry 17% of the exact regret (12.2 of 71.2) and about 1 raw point/game of flip gain above 16 cards.
2. The bonus gap to the clairvoyant bound (3,878) is the cost of not knowing the order (setups cannot be seen coming); the legal part of it that is visible in hindsight (lost windows) is at most 197 points per game before costs, 67 above 16 cards.
3. Honest ceiling for a streak-targeted change: about +2 to +5 points per game raw (GUESS from the 1.1 + 2.7 + hazard-flip bounds), 0.6x of that about +1 to +3. A paired set1000 test (SE about 9-15) cannot resolve that.

## First candidate (bust-aware, not a bonus change; low expected gain, written as asked)
**H1 hazard-gated re-score** (`experiments/streak-hazard/`, nothing built yet; must be self-contained to be frozen). Trigger on the champion's own list at decisions with more than 16 cards left (below that deeper:16 is exact or near-exact) when (i) the top two or three candidates are within 150 points, and (ii) the chosen or a runner-up afterstate has no empty column and no Stay left. For each trigger candidate compute exactly (counts only, no order): hazard = P(next card busts every column) and the 2-card hazard (P that after the best reply there is still no safe column), then re-score the candidate with the champion's 2 chance plies plus the exact hazard term where the network leaf is replaced by (network leaf minus network's implied hazard cost + exact hazard x measured bust cost 1,000 + column loss); play the best. Uses the same network, only reads the observable state. Cost: 434 triggers per 1,000 games, about 5 ms each, so negligible. Expected gain: raw upper bound +4 per game (39 decisions per 1,000 games with a low-hazard alternative within 120 points, each assumed to gain +100: 3.9), realistic +1 to +2 (GUESS); 0.6x-adjusted (Theorist's guess) +0.6 to +1.2. **Not worth a Referee slot alone**; bundle with Search's midgame-search (it already deepens close calls, so H1's trigger set is a subset of what that tests). Test plan: set1000 only, 1,000 games, paired, accepted only at >2 SE; under the 1,000-game cap no larger run is planned.
**Directions the data points to, ranked by measured size (none streak-specific):**
1. No-empty-column decisions and Stay timing above 16 cards: raw net flip gains 8.6 + 9.6 (no empty column, 17-32 and >=33) and 13.6 (13-16) per game, i.e. about 32 raw, about 19 at 0.6x (GUESS factor), mostly in bust-free games; streak-live states are not where it is. Needs search/evaluator work at those states (Search's and the Learner's lanes).
2. The 13-16 card band: deeper:16 solves exactly only 11 / 36 / 60 / 87% of decisions at 16 / 15 / 14 / 13 cards; shipped52's non-exact decisions there are +196 under the realized value (flat), flips 28.6 +- 6.2 raw per game at 13-16. Better fallback evaluation or search there is the largest nearby number (about 17 at 0.6x, GUESS).
3. Evaluator valuation of multi-column setups (3+ strong columns, 2.9 times per game, converting 13% vs 17% hindsight-reachable): calibration shows no detectable bias at +-25..60 points per bucket; collecting exact-labeled setup positions from the Learner's data would be the way to see +-10.
